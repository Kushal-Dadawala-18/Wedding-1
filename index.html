How Our Story Began

Every Love story has a beginning, and ours started with a simple conversation. What began as messages and getting to know each other soon turned into something we both looked forward to.

Even with me being in Germany, the distance didn't stop us. Between different time zones, long chats, a few laughs, and the occasional "Are you still awake?", we slowly started becoming a part of each other's everyday lives.

Eventually, it was time to take things beyond the screen! Our families came together in Surat, and we finally met in person. What followed were a few lovely dates, more conversations, plenty of smiles, and the joy of discovering just how much we enjoyed each other's company.

Somewhere along the way, those initial conversations turned into something much more meaningful. Turns out, all those messages were leading somewhere pretty special!

And now, here we are, ready to turn those first conversations into a lifetime of memories.

Who knew one little chat could lead to forever?
